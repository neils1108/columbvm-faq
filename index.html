ColumbVM FAQ

What is ColumbVM?
ColumbVM is a fork of CollabVM. We are a project where you can control virtual machines online with friends or other people.
Why did I get banned?
Because you broke the ColumbVM's Rules. You are required to read them before using the site.
When do we get pinged?
When something important has happened. Most of the time, we use reaction pings if it isn't important.
When do I get banned?
You get banned when we see your being annoying or when your a threat for core functions of ColumbVM
What if I see a "Admin" or a "Moderator" abuse?
Report it in our Discord server. Give info about the person and screenshots where is shown that the person is abusing.
